Privacy Policy

Last updated 12 September 2026 · Applies to the SENSAI iPhone app

The short version

  • Your training videos stay on your phone. They are never uploaded.
  • To analyse a round, SENSAI sends still frames sampled from that video, plus the few answers you give about the session. They are processed to write your report and are not kept afterwards.
  • Your profile, your reports and your session history never leave your phone.
  • We hold little more than your email address and whether your subscription is active.
  • No advertising, no tracking, no analytics, and nothing sold to anyone.
  • Deleting your account in Settings erases everything on the phone and the account on our servers.

1 Who We Are

SENSAI ("SENSAI", "we", "us", or "our") provides an AI-powered striking coaching app for iPhone, covering boxing, kickboxing and Muay Thai. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Questions about this policy or your data? Email us at sensai.1.slip.2@gmail.com.

2 Information We Collect

  • Account information. Signing in with Apple or Google creates an account. We collect the email address your sign-in provider gives us — a Hide My Email relay address counts as one — along with which provider you used, any basic profile details that provider includes, such as a name or a link to your profile picture, and the dates you created the account and last signed in. Sign-in events, including the IP address they came from, are logged so that suspicious access can be spotted.
  • Training content you submit. When you ask for an analysis, the app sends still frames sampled from the round, together with your stance, the ruleset you train in, whether the round was shadowboxing or bag work, the optional note you type about what you were working on, and how long the clip was. The video file itself is not sent.
  • Training content that stays on your phone. Your videos, your coaching reports, your session history and your profile — first name, stance, sport, usual session type, experience, training frequency, height, weight, the goals you picked and how you heard about the app — are held in the app's private storage on your device and are not transmitted to us.
  • Subscription information. Whether you hold an active subscription, which plan it is, when it renews or expires, and the billing events our subscription provider reports to us. No payment details ever reach us — the store handles the entire transaction.
  • Device and diagnostic information. SENSAI contains no analytics software and no crash-reporting software of its own, and collects no usage statistics. The billing code in the app, which is there to sell and check the subscription, receives basic technical details such as app version, device model, operating system version and the country a request came from. It also records subscription events, such as when the paywall was shown, what was bought and when it renewed.
  • Support communications. Messages you send us by email, including their contents.

3 Your Videos Stay On Your Device

The clip you pick is copied into the app's own storage so a saved analysis still has its footage to play back, and we never receive it. Your phone does the watching: SENSAI uses Apple's on-device body tracking to follow your joints through the round and pick out the moments worth coaching, entirely on the device, with no network involved.

Only the still frames from those moments leave, and only when you ask for an analysis. They are processed in real time to write your feedback and are not retained on our servers afterwards — not the frames, not the session details, and not the report that comes back. We ask the AI model provider not to keep them either; under the commercial terms we operate under, submitted content is not used to train their models, and any copy they hold for safety and abuse monitoring is tied to our account rather than to you. The finished report is saved on your phone, not on our servers. We never sell your media or your personal information.

If you never request an analysis, no footage and no session detail ever leaves your phone.

One thing worth knowing: the app's storage is part of your iPhone, so your videos and reports are included in an iPhone backup, the same as any other app's files. If you use iCloud Backup, Apple holds that backup under your own Apple Account — we have no access to it, and you can leave SENSAI out of it in iOS Settings, under iCloud.

4 How We Use Information

  • To produce your coaching reports from the footage you submit for analysis
  • To keep you signed in, and to tie your subscription to your account
  • To check that you hold an active subscription before an analysis runs
  • To operate, secure, troubleshoot and improve the app
  • To respond to support requests and feature ideas
  • To comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms

We do not use your information to profile you, to advertise to you, or for anything not on this list.

5 Service Providers

We share information only with providers that help us run the service, and only as needed for them to perform that work. These include a cloud hosting and database provider, which holds the account and subscription records and runs the server that analyses are routed through; an AI model provider, which processes the submitted frames and session details to generate your analysis; a subscription management provider, which runs purchases, restores, plan changes and refunds; and the Apple App Store, for distribution and payment.

We do not sell your personal information or share it for cross-context behavioural advertising. There is no advertising network, data broker or analytics company anywhere in this list.

6 Data Retention and Deletion

Everything on your device stays until you remove it. You can delete a single session from the history screen, or delete everything at once in Settings → Delete account.

Delete account erases your profile, every saved report and every stored video from the device, and deletes your account record from our servers, including the record of what you are entitled to. It takes effect immediately and cannot be undone; where a routine backup still holds a copy, it is overwritten as that backup ages out. Deleting the app alone removes only what is on the phone, so use Delete account if you want the server record gone as well.

Some records may be retained where required by law or for fraud prevention: the record of subscription and billing events, which is what any payment dispute is settled from, and which carries no name, email address or payment details; and the security log of sign-in attempts, which our authentication provider keeps for a limited period.

Deleting your account does not cancel a paid subscription. Cancel that in Settings → Manage, or in the App Store under your Apple Account.

7 Your Rights

Depending on where you live — including under the GDPR in the UK and EU, and the CCPA in California — you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete your personal information, to object to certain processing, and to withdraw consent.

Most of what SENSAI knows about you is on your own device, visible in the app and erasable at any time in Settings, and deleting your account does the rest immediately. For anything else, email sensai.1.slip.2@gmail.com and we will respond within one month. You also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

8 Security

We use industry-standard safeguards including encryption in transit, access controls and row-level database security, so that an account can only ever read its own records. Your sign-in credentials are held in the iOS keychain and marked device-only, so a phone restored from a backup signs in again rather than inheriting a session. The keys that can spend money live only on our server and are never shipped inside the app.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to protect your information and to notify you if a breach materially affects you.

9 Children's Privacy

SENSAI is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. Anyone under the age of majority in their region should use the app with the involvement of a parent, guardian or coach. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, email us and we will delete it.

10 International Users

SENSAI is operated from the United States, and your information may be processed in the United States and in other countries where our service providers operate, which may have different data protection laws than your own.

11 Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date above and, for material changes, provide notice in the app's release notes. Continued use of SENSAI after an update means you accept the revised policy.
