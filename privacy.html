Privacy Policy

Last updated [DATE] · Applies to the SENSAI iPhone app

The short version

  • Your training videos stay on your phone. They are never uploaded.
  • To analyse a round, SENSAI sends still frames sampled from that video, plus the three answers you give about the session, to Anthropic's Claude API. That is the only thing that leaves your device.
  • Your name, height, weight, goals, email and session history never leave your phone.
  • There is no SENSAI account server, no tracking, no advertising, and no analytics.
  • Deleting your account in Settings deletes everything, because everything is here.

What stays on your device

SENSAI keeps the following in the app's own private storage on your iPhone. None of it is transmitted to us or to anyone else:

  • Your videos. A clip you choose is copied into the app so a saved analysis still has its footage to play back.
  • Your coaching reports and the session history built from them.
  • Your profile: first name, stance, sport, usual session type, experience, training frequency, height, weight, the goals you picked, and how you said you heard about the app.
  • Your account details: the display name and, if your sign-in provider supplied one, the email address.
  • A sign-in identifier from Apple or Google, held in the iOS keychain so the app can confirm your sign-in is still valid.

We have no copy of any of this. There is no SENSAI server that stores user data, no backup of your sessions, and no way for us to look at your footage.

What leaves your device, and why

When you ask for an analysis, the app samples still images from the round you filmed and sends them, together with a short description of the session, directly to the Claude API operated by Anthropic. That description consists only of:

  • your stance (orthodox or southpaw),
  • the ruleset you train in (boxing, kickboxing or Muay Thai),
  • whether the round was shadowboxing or bag work, and
  • the optional note you type about what you were working on.

The video file itself is never uploaded — only the sampled stills. Your name, email, height, weight, goals, sign-in identifier and past sessions are not included in the request, and no identifier that points back to you is attached to it.

The purpose is the obvious one: producing your coaching report is impossible without showing the coach what you did. Anthropic processes the request to generate that report. Under Anthropic's commercial terms, API inputs and outputs are not used to train their models; Anthropic may retain request content for a limited period for safety and abuse monitoring. Their handling is governed by Anthropic's privacy policy.

If you never request an analysis, nothing ever leaves your phone.

Signing in

You can sign in with Apple, sign in with Google, or skip signing in entirely — the app works the same either way, because an account here is only a name to put on your own sessions.

SENSAI has no account server, so signing in does not create an account with us and sends us nothing. What happens is local: the provider confirms who you are, the app stores the identifier they return in your device's keychain, and keeps the name and email on the device. Your interaction with Apple or Google during sign-in is covered by their own privacy policies. If you choose Sign in with Apple and use Apple's Hide My Email, we never see a real address — nor does anyone, since the address stays on your phone.

Subscriptions and payment

SENSAI Pro is sold through the App Store. We never see your payment details — Apple handles the entire transaction. The app reads only Apple's signed record of whether a subscription is currently active, so it knows whether to unlock. Purchases, refunds and cancellations are managed by Apple under Apple's privacy policy.

What SENSAI does not do

  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers.
  • No tracking across apps or websites, and no data brokers.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting SDKs. The app contains no third-party code of any kind.
  • No selling or sharing of personal information, under any definition, to anyone.
  • No access to your photo library. iOS shows you its own picker, and only hands over the single clip you choose.

How long anything is kept

Everything on your device stays until you remove it. You can delete a single session from the history screen, or delete everything at once in Settings → Delete account, which erases your profile, every saved report and every stored video from the device. Deleting the app removes all of it too. Because there is no server copy, deletion is final — we cannot restore anything for you.

Deleting your account does not cancel a paid subscription. Cancel that in Settings → Manage, or in the App Store under your Apple Account.

Frames already sent for an analysis are held by Anthropic under their retention terms, linked to our API access rather than to you.

Your rights

Depending on where you live — including under the GDPR in the UK and EU, and the CCPA in California — you have rights to access, correct, export and delete personal information held about you, and to object to certain processing.

In SENSAI's case these are unusually direct: we hold nothing, so there is nothing for us to disclose, correct or delete on your behalf. Every piece of information the app has about you is on your own device, visible in the app, and erasable by you at any time in Settings. Where we do process anything — the frames sent for an analysis — the legal basis is performance of the contract, since it is the service you asked for. If you have a request or a complaint, contact [SUPPORT@YOURDOMAIN.COM]; you also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

Children

SENSAI is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has used the app in a way that concerns you, contact us and we will help — though in practice, deleting the app removes everything.

Changes to this policy
