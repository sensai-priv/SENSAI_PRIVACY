Privacy Policy

Last updated 2 September 2026 · Applies to the SENSAI iPhone app

The short version

  • Your training videos stay on your phone. They are never uploaded.
  • To analyse a round, SENSAI sends still frames sampled from that video, plus the three answers you give about the session, through our server to a third-party AI model provider. Neither we nor that provider keeps them.
  • Your name, height, weight, goals and session history never leave your phone.
  • Signing in creates an account on our servers, holding little more than your email address. There is no tracking, no advertising, and no analytics.
  • Deleting your account in Settings erases everything on the phone and deletes the account record from our servers.

What stays on your device

SENSAI keeps the following in the app's own private storage on your iPhone. None of it is transmitted to us or to anyone else:

  • Your videos. A clip you choose is copied into the app so a saved analysis still has its footage to play back.
  • Your coaching reports and the session history built from them.
  • Your profile: first name, stance, sport, usual session type, experience, training frequency, height, weight, the goals you picked, and how you said you heard about the app.
  • Your account details: the display name and, if your sign-in provider supplied one, the email address.
  • A sign-in identifier from Apple or Google, held in the iOS keychain so the app can confirm your sign-in is still valid.

Your footage, your reports and your profile stay here. We keep no copy of any of them, and we cannot look at your training. The only things held on our servers are the account record and the subscription record described below.

What leaves your device, and why

When you ask for an analysis, the app samples still images from the round you filmed and sends them, together with a short description of the session, to our own server, which forwards them to a third-party AI model provider that analyses them on our behalf. That description consists only of:

  • your stance (orthodox or southpaw),
  • the ruleset you train in (boxing, kickboxing or Muay Thai),
  • whether the round was shadowboxing or bag work, and
  • the optional note you type about what you were working on.

The video file itself is never uploaded — only the sampled stills. Your name, email, height, weight, goals, sign-in identifier and past sessions are not included in the request, and nothing that points back to you is attached to what reaches the AI provider.

The request carries your sign-in so our server can check that you have an active subscription and are within the monthly number of analyses a subscription includes. That check is the only reason the request is not anonymous to us. Our server keeps a running count of how many analyses you have run this month; it does not store the frames, the session description, or the report that comes back. All three exist on our side only for the seconds the request takes.

The purpose is the obvious one: producing your coaching report is impossible without showing the coach what you did. The AI provider processes the request to generate that report. Under the commercial API terms we operate under, inputs and outputs are not used to train the provider's models; the provider may retain request content for a limited period for safety and abuse monitoring. Their handling is governed by that provider's own privacy policy.

If you never request an analysis, nothing ever leaves your phone.

Signing in

You can sign in with Apple or with Google. There is no way to use SENSAI without one of the two: your subscription and your monthly allowance are recorded against an account, and without one there would be nothing to restore a purchase to.

Signing in creates an account record on servers we operate through Supabase, our authentication provider. That record holds an account identifier, the email address your sign-in provider supplies, which provider you used, and the dates you created the account and last signed in. Supabase also logs sign-in events, including the IP address the request came from, so that suspicious access can be spotted. Data is stored in the United States (our provider's us-east-1 region).

Supabase processes this on our behalf and does not use it for its own purposes; see Supabase's privacy policy. Your interaction with Apple or Google during sign-in is covered by their own policies. If you use Sign in with Apple with Hide My Email, the relay address is what we store, and we never see your real one.

What is not on our servers: your videos, your coaching reports, your session history, your profile, and your height and weight. Those stay on your device.

Subscriptions and payment

SENSAI Pro is sold through the App Store. We never see your payment details — Apple handles the entire transaction. The app reads only Apple's signed record of whether a subscription is currently active, so it knows whether to unlock. Purchases, refunds and cancellations are managed by Apple under Apple's privacy policy.

Our server keeps its own copy of that answer, tied to your account: Apple's identifier for the subscription, which plan it is, when it expires, and whether it was refunded. Apple tells our server directly when any of that changes. It is what stops a cancelled subscription from carrying on working, and it contains no payment information.

What SENSAI does not do

  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers.
  • No tracking across apps or websites, and no data brokers.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting SDKs. The app contains no third-party code of any kind.
  • No selling or sharing of personal information, under any definition, to anyone.
  • No access to your photo library. iOS shows you its own picker, and only hands over the single clip you choose.

How long anything is kept

Everything on your device stays until you remove it. You can delete a single session from the history screen, or delete everything at once in Settings → Delete account, which erases your profile, every saved report and every stored video from the device.

Delete account also deletes your account record from our servers, including the sign-in logs and your usage count. Deletion is permanent and takes effect immediately; where a routine backup still holds a copy, it is overwritten as that backup ages out. Deleting the app alone removes only what is on the phone, so use Delete account if you want the server record gone as well. We cannot restore anything afterwards.

Deleting your account does not cancel a paid subscription. Cancel that in Settings → Manage, or in the App Store under your Apple Account.

Frames already sent for an analysis are held by the AI provider under their retention terms, linked to our API access rather than to you.

Your rights

Depending on where you live — including under the GDPR in the UK and EU, and the CCPA in California — you have rights to access, correct, export and delete personal information held about you, and to object to certain processing.

Most of what SENSAI knows about you is on your own device, visible in the app and erasable at any time in Settings. For the account and subscription records we do hold, you can request a copy, a correction, or their deletion by writing to sensai.1.slip.2@gmail.com, and we will respond within one month. Deleting your account in the app does the same thing immediately. Our legal basis for holding the account record is performance of the contract, since it is what lets you sign in; for the frames sent for an analysis, the same. You also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

Children

SENSAI is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has used the app in a way that concerns you, contact us and we will help — though in practice, deleting the app removes everything.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes what it does with information, this policy changes with it, and the date at the top is updated. Material changes will be noted in the app's release notes.

Contact

Questions about this policy, or about anything the app does with your information, go to sensai.1.slip.2@gmail.com.
